Terrain tools

Click the map to set a point or place a planned repeater. Locations and antenna heights affect the estimates.

Coverage and point-to-point
Repeater plan

Known repeaters need advertised locations. Their antenna height starts at 10 m; adjust it if known. Bulk coverage may take time and appears as each layer completes. Set a search radius in miles, choose the area button, then click the map to select known contacts, repeaters, rooms and sensors within that distance. The coverage radius setting applies separately to each node. Other planned nodes stay on the map but are excluded until selected.

Or select “New planned repeater” above and click the map. Drag markers to adjust positions. Select repeaters for coverage and links and set their antenna heights. Link calculations support up to 20 selected repeaters.
